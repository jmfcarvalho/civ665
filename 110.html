MODULE 01 — COMPUTATIONAL INVENTORY FRAMEWORK

Inventory Modeling & LCI Matrix Compilation

Computational Technology Matrix Inversion

Translates interconnected unit processes into standardized input-output matrices, solving upstream environmental interventions via linear algebra.

$$\mathbf{A} \cdot \mathbf{s} = \mathbf{f} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \mathbf{s} = \mathbf{A}^{-1} \cdot \mathbf{f} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \mathbf{g} = \mathbf{B} \cdot \mathbf{s} = \mathbf{B} \cdot \mathbf{A}^{-1} \cdot \mathbf{f}$$
Technology Matrix ($\mathbf{A}$): Inter-process economic matrix detailing flows between unit processes required to produce 1 functional unit.
Intervention Matrix ($\mathbf{B}$): Elementary flows crossed with processes: virgin resource extractions and direct environmental emissions ($\mathbf{g}$).
Computational Formalism: Heijungs & Suh (2002); ISO 14044:2006 Linear Input-Output Scaling
LCI DATABASES

Background Datasets & Regional Bias

Ecoinvent & US LCI: Global commercial reference datasets; require regional energy grid and transport adaptations for developing nations.
National Repositories (e.g. SICV Brasil): Calibrated regional inventories reflect local electricity mix decarbonization and distinct raw mineral supply chains.

MODULE 02 — MULTI-OUTPUT PROCESS ALLOCATION

Co-Product Allocation in Industrial Binders

ISO 14044 Multi-Output Allocation Hierarchy

Resolving joint environmental burdens across industrial by-products (GGBS, Fly Ash, Silica Fume) that substitute carbon-intensive clinker.

$$\Phi_i^{\text{mass}} = \frac{m_i}{\sum_{j} m_j} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \Phi_i^{\text{econ}} = \frac{m_i \cdot p_i}{\sum_{j} (m_j \cdot p_j)} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad I_i = \Phi_i \cdot I_{\text{system}}$$
Step 1: Allocation Avoidance: Subdivide unit processes or expand system boundaries through avoided burdens (substitution method).
Step 2: Physical / Economic Split: Partition impacts by physical causation (mass, stoichiometry) or market economic revenue ($\Phi^{\text{econ}}$).
Normative Compliance: ISO 14044:2006 (§4.3.4.2); EN 15804+A2 Partitioning Sensitivity
BY-PRODUCT DILEMMA

Mass vs. Economic Vulnerability

Blast Furnace Slag (GGBS): Mass allocation burdens slag with ~20–25% of crude steel blast furnace emissions; economic allocation lowers this burden to ~1–3%.
Coal Fly Ash & Silica Fume: Often modeled as zero-burden waste at origin (cut-off approach), only charging secondary beneficiation, drying, and grinding.

MODULE 03 — SOFTWARE HIERARCHY & EPD GOVERNANCE

3-Level Software Architecture & Type III Declarations

Computational Decision Hierarchy (Bayer et al., 2010)

LCA deployment in civil engineering transitions from unit material formulation to complete whole-building life-cycle performance.

$$\text{EPD}_{\text{result}} = \sum_{m} \left( \sum_{i \in \{\text{A1-A3}\}} \mathbf{B}_i \cdot \mathbf{s}_i \right) + \mathbf{LCIA}_{\text{A4-C4}} \quad \left[\frac{\text{Impact Unit}}{\text{Declared Unit}}\right]$$
Level 1: Materials Comparison: Specialized process modeling platforms (GaBi, SimaPro, OpenLCA, TEAM) linking bill-of-materials to unit LCI datasets.
Level 2: Whole-Building Tools: Design-stage assembly tools (Athena Impact Estimator, LISA, Envest) integrating geometry and component take-offs.
Normative Framework: ISO 21930:2017 & EN 15804:2012+A2:2019 Multi-Tier Decision Systems
STANDARDIZED DECLARATIONS

EPD Verification & Level 3 Rating

Product Category Rules (PCR): Mandates harmonized functional units, system cut-off boundaries, allocation principles, and background database age.
Level 3: Green Building Certification: Credit-based compliance engines (LEED, BREEAM, DGNB) granting materials credits for third-party verified Type III EPDs.
Direct transition to the Synthesis & Dual Assessment Module.

EPD PROGRAM OPERATORS & SOFTWARE TAXONOMY

Standardized EPD Verification & Software Levels

Level 1 (Materials):

Scientific parameter modeling: GaBi (Sphera), SimaPro (PRé), OpenLCA, and TEAM. Used by researchers and manufacturers to compute unit LCI matrices, sensitivity scenarios, and EPD core datasets.

Level 2 (Building Systems):

Design-phase assessment: Athena Impact Estimator (Canada), LISA (Australia), and Envest (BRE-UK). Aggregates structural assemblies, frames, and envelope bills into building-level environmental profiles.

Level 3 (Rating Frameworks):

Prescriptive green rating: LEED v4.1 (USGBC), BREEAM (BRE Global), and Green Star. Awards credit points based on EPD availability, regional supply verification, and whole-building LCA carbon savings.

EPD Component Standard Reference Mandatory Content
PCR Document ISO 14025 / EN 15804 Functional unit (e.g. 1 m³ of concrete), declared stages (A1-A3, C1-C4), and allocation rules.
Third-Party Verifier ISO 14025 §8.1 Independent panel audit certifying LCI data quality, mass balance completeness, and cut-off compliance.
Program Operator ISO 21930 / IBU / EPDInt Publishes the registered declaration into public databases, preventing unverified proprietary green claims.
EPDs do not certify that a product is green; they provide scientifically verified environmental metrics.
