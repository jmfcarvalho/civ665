COURSE INSTRUCTOR & SCIENTIFIC LEAD

Prof. José Maria Franco de Carvalho

Associate Professor | CNPq PQ-C Researcher | Coordinator of PMS & PPGEC — UFV

Academic Profile & Scientific Leadership

Civil Engineer graduated from the Federal University of Viçosa - UFV (2004), Master of Science in Civil Engineering from the Federal University of Ouro Preto - UFOP (2007), and Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from the Federal University of Ouro Preto with a doctoral research internship (sandwich period) at the Federal Institute for Materials Research and Testing in Berlin - BAM, Germany (2019).

He is currently an Associate Professor at the Federal University of Viçosa (UFV), a CNPq Research Productivity Fellow (Level PQ-C), General Coordinator of the Prototyping Center for Materials, Parts, and Systems (PMS/UFV), Coordinator of the Graduate Program in Civil Engineering (PPGEC/UFV), and Co-Leader of the Sustainable & Innovative Construction Research Group (SICon - CNPq/UFV). He actively collaborates with CNPq research groups RECICLOS (UFOP), ATIVE (UFOP), DEC UFV Structures, and TechBIM (UFV).

Primary Technological & Research Focus: Construction processes and materials technology, with emphasis on construction materials and structures, working on the following topics: sustainable materials, eco-efficient concrete, reuse of industrial waste, cement technology and cement-based composites, and maintenance and repair of road infrastructure.

PEDAGOGICAL & TECHNICAL DISCLOSURE

Statement on the Use of Artificial Intelligence

In line with academic integrity, transparency, and scientific rigor, instructional materials, web interfaces, and interactive technical components for this course were developed through collaborative human-AI integration using state-of-the-art Large Language Models.

1. Interface Engineering & Interactive Tools: Generative models supported the implementation of lightweight front-end code, responsive layouts, dynamic data visualizations, and interactive engineering calculation modules.
2. Content Structuring & Didactic Scaffolding: AI assistance was employed to synthesize, organize, and revise instructional content and visual layouts, working directly from the original course materials and under the continuous guidance of the course instructor.
3. Academic Oversight & Intellectual Responsibility: All the contents were curated, verified, and validated by the instructor, who retains full pedagogical and intellectual responsibility for the course content.
