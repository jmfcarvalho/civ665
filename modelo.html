THEORETICAL MODULE

Pure Bending Stress Equation

Classical Formulation

The normal stress distribution $\sigma(y)$ in a symmetric cross-section subjected to bending moment $M$ is expressed as:

$$\sigma(y) = \frac{M \cdot y}{I_x}$$

Where $I_x$ denotes the principal centroidal moment of inertia and $y$ represents the distance from the neutral axis.
