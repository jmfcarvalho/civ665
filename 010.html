GRADING SYSTEM

Assessment Breakdown

40%

Quizzes & Weekly Pitches

In-class interactive quizzes (attendance tracking) + weekly off-class quizzes with 3-minute video pitch submissions.

30%

Literature Seminars

Executed in trios. Presentation of conceptual core + critical analysis of at least two recent scientific papers.

30%

Final Scientific Paper

Group project (max 6 authors). Submission of full manuscript PDF + 30-minute video defense with mandatory full team participation.

OPERATIONAL DYNAMICS

Class Execution & Modalities

Hybrid Course Delivery

  • In-Person Lectures: Mandatory for UFV full-time scholarship/regular students.
  • Synchronous Online Access: Dedicated for international students and non-scholarship part-time UFV students.

Class Structure (Parts 1, 2, 3)

  • Part 1: Expository lectures delivered by the professor.
  • Part 2: Student-led seminars, featuring conceptual synthesis + critical review of two recent papers, followed by professor-guided debate.
  • Part 3: Supervised research paper development in teams (up to 6 authors).

SUBMISSION WORKFLOW

Quizzes, Pitches & Final Defense

Weekly Video Pitch Dynamics

Submitted via Google Forms alongside off-class questionnaires:

  • • Maximum duration: 3 minutes.
  • • Video hosted in student's personal repository (Drive/YouTube/Vimeo) with accessible link provided.
  • • In-class quizzes verify real-time conceptual comprehension and attendance.

Final Paper Defense (Part 3)

Single Google Forms submission per group:

  • • Full manuscript PDF (complete formatting, citations, references).
  • • 30-minute video presentation/defense featuring all team members in a single video link.
  • • Hosted on student repository, permanently accessible for professor evaluation.
